Install to your home screen (optional)

  • iPhone / iPad: open the app in Safari → Share → "Add to Home Screen" for full offline, full-screen use.
  • Desktop Chrome / Edge: use the "Install" icon in the address bar.
  • No install needed — it works the same directly in your browser.

Migrating from the old address

If you previously used Eigentime at wowayou.github.io/time-logger/: browser data is isolated per origin, so your records won't follow automatically. On the old address's ··· menu, use "Store backup" to export a full JSON backup, then use "Import" in the ··· menu of the new app — every record is validated and previewed for conflicts before anything is imported.

Data boundary

  • All records live only in your browser (localStorage) — nothing is uploaded, synced, or tied to an account.
  • This site loads no analytics, ads, or third-party scripts.
  • A full backup can be copied, downloaded, or shared as JSON at any time; move between devices with "Export → Import" — imports are validated and merged record by record.
  • Clearing your browser's site data deletes your records — export a backup regularly.
  • Read the full Privacy Policy.

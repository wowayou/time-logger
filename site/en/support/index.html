A thank-you card

Keep it or share it with someone who might find the tool useful. It contains no name, payment details or supporter number.

Save thank-you card · SVG image

If your phone opens the image first, save a screenshot or use the browser share menu to save it to Files.

Use a pack in Rotating lines

  1. Download a pack. Each has 14 editable lines, one per line.
  2. In the app, open “··· → Motto · Rotating lines” and choose “Rotating lines”.
  3. Choose “Import from file”, review the lines, then save. Import appends to your library; clear the text field first if you want to replace it.
  4. One line appears each day, in order, and stays the same that day. Your library is included in full backups.
